#### [SCOPE_CREEP] ADVISORY
- Location: plan.md § Implementation Tasks task 4 (plan.md:140); plan.md § Impact (plan.md:107); decision-log.md § Review Findings, "[plan-review] The `resultType` discriminator gained a second decision site"
- Issue: the interview constrained the plan to "no new public method (no new ADBC-facing API, no new public accessor beyond what is needed for internal reachability and testability)". Task 4 adds three new public items — `pub enum ResultEntryKind`, `ResultSetInfo::kind`, `PreparedStatementResponseData::result_set_columns` — and these are genuinely public: `pub mod transport` (`src/lib.rs:102`) and `pub mod messages` (`src/transport/mod.rs:44`) make both host types reachable from the crate root. The stated justification is "`pub(crate)` would trip `dead_code` in a default-features `cargo build`". That premise is correct — `src/transport/websocket.rs` is `#[cfg(feature = "websocket")]` (`src/transport/mod.rs:51-52`), so the only caller vanishes under default features — but the remedy is disproportionate and buys nothing the plan verifies. `dead_code` is a warning, not an error: no `RUSTFLAGS=-Dwarnings` exists in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, and the only `-D warnings` gate is `cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features` (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:81`), where `websocket` is on and the lint cannot fire. plan.md § Verification § Checklist's Lint row uses `--all-features` too. So `pub` widens the public surface to suppress a lint that neither CI nor the plan's own checklist observes. `#[cfg_attr(not(feature = "websocket"), allow(dead_code))]` on `pub(crate)` items resolves it with no surface change and no `cfg`-gated code path. Separately, § Impact says "Two additive public methods appear on the wire-message types" and omits `ResultEntryKind`, a new public type.
- Fix: In plan.md task 4, change `pub enum ResultEntryKind`, `pub fn kind`, and `pub fn result_set_columns` to `pub(crate)`, and require `#[cfg_attr(not(feature = "websocket"), allow(dead_code))]` on the two methods and the enum with the one-line reason "no in-crate caller under default features; `websocket.rs` is the only consumer". Replace the "Both methods are `pub`, not `pub(crate)`" sentence with that instruction. In plan.md § Impact, delete the "Two additive public methods" paragraph. Update the decision-log entry's Direction change to record `pub(crate)` plus the `cfg_attr`, and state that clippy runs `--all-features`, so the lint never reaches CI. If the human prefers `pub` after all, keep it but add `ResultEntryKind` to the § Impact list.
