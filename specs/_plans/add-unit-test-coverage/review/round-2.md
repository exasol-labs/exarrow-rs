#### [UNSTATED_ASSUMPTION] ADVISORY

- Issue: the projection that justifies the 82.0 percent floor states its sensitivity backwards. The plan says the projection is "82.5 percent, or 82.47 percent under a pessimistic test-code assumption" and "falls to 82.47 percent if the new tests are denser than the crate's current 1.81 test lines per covered production line".
